DOES THE LLM TEACHER ACTUALLY TEACH THE STUDENT?  (ablation, 4 seeds)
==========================================================================

Setup: identical pipeline, identical data, identical hyper-parameters. The ONLY
difference is whether the 89 rows from the LLM teacher (data/traces_llm/train.jsonl,
weighted x3 = 267 of 36,783 training rows = 0.7%) are in the mix. Scored on the
ns-3 test split, minimum-expected-cost decision, excluding the held-out `sweep`
family (which no net can get and which would swamp the comparison).

 seed   oracle only   +LLM rows     delta
    0        84.96%      88.10%    +3.14%
    1        89.20%      86.59%    -2.61%
    2        85.49%      86.59%    +1.10%
    3        87.99%      86.93%    -1.06%

  mean delta  +0.14 points
  std  delta  2.51 points
  range       -2.61 .. +3.14

RESULT: +0.14 +/- 2.18 points. The LLM teacher's contribution to the student NET is
indistinguishable from zero. The +3.15 at seed 0 -- which is the seed train_mixed.py
hardcodes, and therefore the only number a single run would ever have shown -- is
seed variance, not signal. Congestion moved 57%->76% at seed 0 and 77%->74% at seed 1.

SECOND FINDING, arguably the more important one: seed-to-seed spread on the headline
ex-sweep accuracy is 84.96% .. 89.20%, a range of 4.2 points with NO change to the
data or the model. Every accuracy figure in this project carries a +/-2 point error
bar that none of the artefacts report. Two runs differing by less than that are not
different.

WHY THE LLM CONTRIBUTION IS ZERO, AND WHAT WOULD CHANGE IT
  89 rows against 36,783 is 0.24% of the mix (0.7% after the x3 weight). No amount of
  correctness in 89 rows moves a model fitted on 36,000. The traces also cover only 5
  of 8 families (fading 36, sweep 23, spot 18, node_loss 8, barrage 4) -- reactive,
  congestion and hidden_term, which are exactly the weak families, have ZERO LLM rows.
  To make the design's distillation claim true the corpus needs thousands of rows
  across all eight families, which is a real LLM spend, not a flag.

WHERE THE LLM DEMONSTRABLY *DOES* DECIDE POLICY
  data/policy_v3.json -- provenance "claude-cli induction, held-out validated". Six
  rules, induced by the LLM from traces, gated at 0.95 held-out precision. Those rules
  are what takes the held-out `sweep` family from 0% (net alone) to 47% (hybrid, 30
  episodes). That is a measured LLM contribution to the deployed policy, and it is the
  one that survives an ablation.

  So the accurate claim is NOT "an LLM teacher distilled into the student net". It is
  "an LLM induced the transferable rule layer; the net was distilled from the oracle".
  Those are different sentences and only the second one is currently supported.
